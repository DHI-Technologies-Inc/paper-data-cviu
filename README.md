# Data and result files for two DHI papers

Result files and scripts that support the numbers in two manuscripts by Dev Sanghvi (DHI Technologies, Inc., Houston, TX; dev@dhi-tech.com).

| Folder | Paper | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| `p07_amodal_counting/` | Inference-Aligned Detectability Calibration and Conformal Count Intervals | multi-seed alignment script, figure script, 14 JSON result files (the supplementary archive of the submission) |
| `p11_face_free_identity/` | Protocol-Locked Appearance Retrieval on a Clothes-Change Video Benchmark: Per-Query Evidence for a Label-Free Reranking Trade-off | protocol lock, locked aggregate metrics, per-query and identity-cluster statistics, result files of the later runs |

Not included: third-party images and videos, annotations of third-party benchmarks, model weights and feature embeddings. The public benchmarks are obtained from their providers under their own terms.

Released for peer review and verification of the two papers. No licence is granted beyond that; write to dev@dhi-tech.com for other uses.
